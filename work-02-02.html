Scaling Design & Operations Aligning Agent-workforce skill, The outcome factory and Echo AI

When everyone is experimenting, consistency is the first casualty. An internal initiative to bring shared thinking to AI-driven product design — defining when systems act, when they recommend, and when humans stay in control. A common foundation so teams could move fast without losing direction. Structure for speed. Intention behind experimentation.

The Gap Between Strategy and Execution

High-level strategic goals (Outcome Factory) often face friction during tactical execution. The reality is that manual workflows limit our ability to iterate at the speed of our ambition.

The goal is to move from "manual task management" to "autonomous outcome delivery." This allows us to scale output without compromising design quality.

Defining the Ecosystem

The solution acts across three distinct layers to ensure strategy translates flawlessly into execution:

  • Outcome Factory (How much?): Provides the "measurement layer" defining success metrics and business impact.
  • Echo AI (What & Why?): Provides the "intelligence layer" offering predictive insights, pattern recognition, and context-aware recommendations.
  • AW_Skill (How?): Acts as the "execution layer." It takes the intelligence from Echo AI and executes the tasks required to hit the KPIs defined by the Outcome Factory.

Building the Intelligent Layer

To enable the AI Policy agent, we built a system where a single text prompt acts as an input, generating a multi-step, interactive conversational UI. This input relies on five foundational documents:

  • Research File (.md): Synthesizes research on "Firewall Agentic Squad: Opportunities, customer expectations."
  • Mapping Matrix (.md): Maps projects and tasks based on the prompt following the core strategy thumb rules.
  • Cisco Persona (.md): Provides context using the JTBD framework for the role (e.g., knowing the difference between a Security Admin and a Network Engineer).
  • AI Playbook (.md): Identifies necessary "Agentic Moments" and Principles (Trigger, Reasoning, Handoff) for the specific task.
  • Figma Components: A UI library for the skill to develop an interactive prototype.

Interactive Prototype

The resulting prototype showcases how the AI policy agent interacts with the user, ensuring complex multi-step workflows are automated effectively.

The Larger Picture & Next Steps

This framework unlocks several benefits for the wider organization:

  • Rapid Prototyping: Designers can "vibe-check" a workflow in React before committing to high-fidelity Figma work.
  • Stakeholder Alignment: We can quickly align with stakeholders using interactive prototypes.
  • Future Proofing: As Cisco agents become more autonomous, logic can be tuned via the .md files without rewriting the entire design system.
  • Portfolio Mapping: Ensures agent behavior matches Cisco's strategic portfolio goals.
