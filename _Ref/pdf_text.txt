PROBLEM
Even during the pandemic year, the number of cyber-attacks and breaches 
increased across the globe, including India. The nature of breaches has 
changed in the recent years, leading to a change of focus from the 
perimeter to threats inside the network. The Zero Trust model has gained 
prominence in terms of approach to Security, knowing that the perimeter is 
dead. 
The journey to incorporate and implement a Zero Trust model requires a 
paradigm change and a journey involving the right tools and technologies.
▪
The threat environment is now everywhere. 
▪
Digitization of businesses has increased the security risk of enterprise 
assets.
▪
Traditional solutions are being challenged due to technological 
evolution.
DESIGN PROBLEM:
Simplifying End User Deployment and Access
During the go-market for Stealth(core) , we received a muted feedback 
from our sales folks to the tune of  - “Does our application lack a wow factor”
With years of investment in the traditional fort and moat 
approach, focusing on the perimeter, the adoption of Zero Trust 
requires a paradigm shift, and a well-planned journey. The need 
is to
Simplify the journey towards Zero Trust without disrupting 
existing models.
Bring in efficiency and speed to recognize and tackle threats.
