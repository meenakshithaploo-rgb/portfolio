A digital platform connecting customers and project managers through every stage of home design and decoration — from empty shell to finished home.

  • Digital Trasformation
  • TCS

The Challenge

B&Q is the market leader in China and one of the largest Western retailers in the country. B&Q wanted to create an Online platform suite which enables their customers to participate, view, and interact with their project managers during the full design and decoration service of their apartments, which helps customers transform an empty concrete shell into a stylish home.

What I Did

  • Target audience analysis for Retail market in China.
  • Consulting for creating role-based sites and sub-sites to bring B&Q execution team and customers close.
  • Consulted and created the front end low fidelity prototyping for main sub sites and dashboards for Community, Blogs, Decoration centre, Sales and project management to help the stakeholders to come up with final RFP.
  • Successfully provided a fitment solution for functionality and features for online products.
