AIOps for Firewalls From Reactive Firefighting to Proactive Intelligence

Complexity had outpaced human intervention. Dashboards, alarm floods, and manual fixes were no longer enough. A ground-up design, now across two phases — each expanding the experience and the use cases it covers. From establishing the core operational model to refining interactions and introducing new scenarios, the design has evolved alongside the product. The through-line: make AI-driven action feel controlled, not opaque. Keep humans in the loop where judgment matters most. A design still evolving — from firefighting to foresight.

  • Enterprise UX
  • Network Security
  • Cisco

The Challenge & Outcome

Managing and monitoring Next-Generation Firewalls (NGFWs) poses a significant challenge due to the overwhelming volume of alerts and logs generated. This complexity hampers administrators' ability to prioritize and address critical issues, leading to inefficiencies in firewall management and potential security risks.

AIOps for Cisco Secure Firewall empowers administrators to efficiently manage NGFWs by providing proactive insights and actionable recommendations. The solution delivers predictive analytics, performance optimization, and intelligent automation to streamline operations and enhance the overall security posture.

Approach: UX Framework & Content Strategy

Our design approach was centered on delivering actionable insights through a clear, multi-layered framework.

  • Sense of Urgency: Clear alerts and color-coded severity indicators prioritize urgent tasks.
  • Intelligent Recommendations: AI-driven solutions offer suggestions based on historical data analysis.
  • Data Visualization Framework: Presenting data in digestible formats tailored to user needs.
  • Progressive Disclosure: Content organized as Bite (Alerts), Snack (Summary), and Meal (Remediation).

Deep Dive: The "Elephant Flow" Use Case

We focused on a critical use case where "elephant flows"—extremely large data flows occupying a disproportionate share of bandwidth—cause elevated CPU usage and performance disruptions. Our AIOps workflow preemptively mitigates risks by detecting these flows and allowing users to bypass or throttle them seamlessly.

Top AIOps Insights

The dashboard highlights priority insights, instantly bringing attention to an observed elephant flow spike.

Analysis and Probable Cause

Administrators can drill down into the analysis to see the specific applications causing high CPU usage and bandwidth consumption, along with a detailed explanation of the probable cause.

Intelligent Remediation

The system recommends tailored remediation steps, such as bypassing or throttling traffic for trusted applications, directly from the insights screen.

Reflection & Roadmap

Research showed that users found the AIOps dashboard highly valuable for instant issue reporting and appreciated the detailed visibility into root cause analysis and remediation steps. Moving forward, the roadmap focuses on:

  • Collaborating with stakeholders to validate additional use cases with customers.
  • Refining metrics using Amplitude to measure the impact of intelligent recommendations.
  • Working closely with the Research team to ensure the AI-driven solutions are continuously optimized.
