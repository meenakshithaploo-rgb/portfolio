Unisys Stealth Redesign Stealth Core and Indentity

Unisys Stealth(core)™ - UI Redesign & Capability Expansion Stealth(core) had the right technology. The experience needed to catch up. We modernized a complex, organically grown security interface while integrating new capabilities - micro-segmentation, adaptive security controls, and expanded Zero Trust coverage across hybrid cloud and IoT. The result: a unified surface bringing visibility, control, and response together - giving security teams clarity, speed, and confidence where it matters most.

The Context & Team

Stealth(core) is a key product under the Unisys Stealth suite of products, which provides identity-based micro-segmentation to transform your existing network into a Zero Trust network, where environments include on-premises and hybrid cloud and end points include IoT devices and containers, data in transit, and so on.

What can you do with Stealth?

  • Know your network: Visualize, model, and design informed security
  • Cloak critical assets: Cryptographically hide critical assets from adversaries
  • Easy micro-segmentation: Isolate assets, regardless of environment, network, or device
  • Enable adaptive security: Integrate existing security tools to correlate, detect, and respond

The Challenge & Opportunity

Even during the pandemic year, the number of cyber-attacks and breaches increased across the globe. The nature of breaches has changed, leading to a change of focus from the perimeter to threats inside the network. The Zero Trust model has gained prominence in terms of approach to Security, knowing that the perimeter is dead.

Design Problem: Simplifying End User Deployment and Access

During the go-market for Stealth(core), we received a muted feedback from our sales folks to the tune of - “Does our application lack a wow factor?” With years of investment in the traditional fort and moat approach, the adoption of Zero Trust requires a paradigm shift, and a well-planned journey.

The Opportunity

  • Simplify the journey towards Zero Trust without disrupting existing models.
  • Bring in efficiency and speed to recognize and tackle threats.

Scope & Involvement

This 5-month project was split into initial research, strategy and envisioning phase followed by Agile-based product rollout.

As a Design Lead on this project, my role started at the design effort scoping stage where I worked closely with business and engineering stakeholders to advise and define the user-centered design approach and identify desired business outcomes.

I directly managed the design team, set design direction and guided the team throughout the process while meeting the product development timelines, and led key design presentations to business and engineering stakeholders.

New UI/UX features for Stealth 6.0:

  • New Stealth Security Dashboards developed with Grafana
  • New Stealth configuration wizards
  • New Stealth modeling canvas design and look & feel
  • New Dark and Light Modes
  • New processing icons with AI messaging along with success toaster popups
