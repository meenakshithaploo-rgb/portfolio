About

Hi, I'm Meenakshi Thaploo!

I'm a design leader with 20+ years building products and teams across cybersecurity, fintech, enterprise commerce, and AI — in the US, Australia, and India.

At Cisco, I lead a global team of 40+ spanning product design, research, design systems, content, and operations across Network Security, Cloud Protection, and AI Defense. Over the last two years I led the org through a deliberate AI transformation — cutting prototype cycles from two weeks to three days — and shipped AI-native products including AI Defense and Hypershield from zero to alpha. The fundamentals didn't change: understand your users, prototype rapidly, iterate on feedback, and create experiences that feel effortless even when the underlying technology is sophisticated.

My specific edge today is Agentic UX. I've designed and shipped production multi-agent interfaces and understand where the seams are — trust signals, progressive disclosure, human-in-the-loop design, and what it actually takes to make AI feel genuinely useful rather than alarming to enterprise users. I'm deeply curious about how AI can amplify human creativity rather than replace it, and I apply that lens to every product decision I make.

Before Cisco, I scaled a 30-person Design CoE at Unisys and delivered enterprise UX for the World Bank Group, IMF, and Mattel. Over two decades I've moved design organizations from tactical execution to strategic business influence — building diverse, autonomous teams where design thinking bridges complex technical requirements with real human needs.

I stay close to the craft. I prototype, I build, and I make the case for design in the language CPOs and engineering leads actually care about. I believe the best innovations come from teams with varied perspectives working toward shared goals — and I've built my leadership practice around that conviction.
