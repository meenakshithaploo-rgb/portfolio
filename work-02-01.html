Design Systems Style Guides and components

Design systems are reusable components and guidelines that help teams design and build digital products more efficiently and consistently. They serve as a single source of truth for design tokens, components, and usage patterns, ensuring scalability, accessibility, and brand consistency across products.

As the founding Design Leader at Unisys UI/UX practice, I established the strategic benefits of a standardized look-and-feel and reusability with senior management. I was provided special funding and initiated a Design System project in 2019.

Research Approach

Internal Audits

  • Step 1: Audit of all components, patterns and features our 7 product lines currently use.
  • Step 2: Audit of products and solutions which are in end-of-life product mode and de-scoped products and tools.

Findings: We utilize components, patterns and features that include common UI components such as data tables, buttons, scheduler, wizards, navigation elements.

External Analysis

  • Step 3: Analysis of the other Design Systems – external (Shopify’s Polaris, Google’s Material Design, IBM’s Carbon, VMware’s Clarity).
  • Step 4: Collaborate with PMs and Engineering leaders to share my roadmap and to understand their roadmap. Refine the document.

Findings: Additional patterns and approaches were identified. We created a document of currently used processes, features and UI components across all our existing products.

Ideation & Defining a Design Vision

The ideation phase focused on defining a robust design vision encompassing a visual design language for Web, iOS, and Android. We explored component and pattern evolution, scale scenarios, and use-cases to establish a single source of truth for UI patterns, specifications, documentations, and code.
